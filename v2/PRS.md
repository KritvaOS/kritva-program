# Product Requirements (PRS)

**Owner of:** product requirements and acceptance expectations.
**Status:** SKELETON — requirements have not yet been written or approved. No requirement is invented here.

## Conventions

- Requirement IDs: `PRS-<AREA>-<NNN>` (area codes assigned when requirements are drafted).
- Each requirement states: statement, rationale, priority, owning workstream, acceptance method (software-only vs physical), and status.
- Every approved requirement must appear in [REQUIREMENT_TRACEABILITY.md](REQUIREMENT_TRACEABILITY.md).
- Physical-hardware requirements are recorded but flagged HARDWARE-BLOCKED while mechanical work is on HOLD.

## Process

1. Draft requirements from existing repository evidence ([REPOSITORY_BASELINE.md](REPOSITORY_BASELINE.md)) and product scope ([PRODUCT_SCOPE.md](PRODUCT_SCOPE.md)).
2. Review in the P0 FOUNDATION milestone; Studio-specific requirements are expanded in P1 STUDIO.
3. Approve via [DECISIONS.md](DECISIONS.md).
