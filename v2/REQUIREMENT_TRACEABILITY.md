## Rules

- Every approved requirement has at least one row; every task cites a requirement.
- Evidence type must be explicit; simulation evidence never satisfies a physical-safety requirement.
- Orphans (requirements without tasks, tasks without requirements) are flagged in [tracking/STATUS.md](tracking/STATUS.md).
